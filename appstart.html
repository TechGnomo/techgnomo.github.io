The problem

Ideas are easy. Choosing the right first version is not.

First-time founders often begin with too many features, choose tools before understanding the problem, underestimate costs and skip validation entirely.

Generic AI assistants can make that worse by producing impressive-looking plans without challenging weak assumptions. AppStart was designed to be useful before it is agreeable.

The approach

Clarity before code.

Every conversation moves through a deliberate product workflow.

01

Understand

Define the user, problem, current alternative, budget, skills and timeline.

02

Validate

Challenge demand, frequency, willingness to pay, distribution and feasibility.

03

Focus

Separate the essential MVP from useful later features and unnecessary scope.

04

Plan

Recommend the technology, monetisation model, roadmap and immediate next action.

Product principles

Designed to resist AI hype.

⌁

Small enough to test

The first version validates the central product assumption instead of imitating a mature competitor.

◇

Honesty over hype

No guaranteed profit, delivery date, app-store approval or invented market evidence.

↗

Teach while building

Every recommendation includes enough context for the user to make an informed decision.
