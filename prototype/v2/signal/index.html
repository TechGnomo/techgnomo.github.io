Signal

One lamp. It marks live, selected, and status. It does not paint the page.

Chosen · #FF5C9A

A luminous rose. On graphite it reads as a lit indicator, not as the v1 text-magenta and not as ClearMoneyPath blue. Ink is never set on the lamp itself.

Void 6.8:1 · bench 6.4:1 · plate 5.8:1 · raised 5.1:1

V1 magenta · #BA0068

Worked on the light bench. On graphite it falls to 3.1:1 on void and 2.9:1 on the bench. Too dim to be a lamp, and it fails AA for text.

Ember · #FF5A36

Passes, 4.8:1 on raised and 6.3:1 on void. It pulls the machine toward red heat. That is the performance cliché this system is not copying.

Amber · #FFB020

8.1:1 on raised. It reads as caution tape or a gold watch. Not a future signal, and not ours.

Beside ClearMoneyPath · #2D7BFF
