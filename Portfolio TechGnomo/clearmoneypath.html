ClearMoneyPath is a mobile-first pay cycle budgeting app built for people who need a clear view of their money before payday arrives. It helps users plan income, bills, debt payments, savings targets, reserves and safe-to-spend money in one simple flow.

This project started from a real personal budgeting workflow and is being developed into a commercial iOS and Android app with authentication, cloud backup and future subscription features.

The problem it solves

Many budgeting tools show what was spent after the damage is done. ClearMoneyPath is designed around the moment that matters most: payday. It answers the question users actually ask: “After everything I need to pay, how much is safe to spend?”

💸

Payday confusion

Users often receive income, pay bills, move money around and only later realise the budget is tighter than expected.

💳

Debt pressure

Credit cards, loans and buy-now-pay-later repayments can be hard to prioritise without a clear payment strategy.

🧾

Irregular costs

Rego, insurance, subscriptions and annual bills create stress when they are not broken down into manageable pay cycle amounts.

Designed around pay cycles, not generic monthly budgets.

ClearMoneyPath is built for weekly, fortnightly and monthly earners who want practical guidance before they spend. The app focuses on real cash flow, not complicated financial jargon.

✓
Income planning
Track main payday income, extra income and one-off cash payments.
✓
Bills and due dates
Organise recurring bills, mark them as paid and see what is still due.
✓
Debt payoff focus
Use snowball, avalanche or custom strategies to decide what to attack first.
✓
Safe-to-spend calculation
See the amount left after must-pay items, reserves, savings and logged spending.

MVP features

The current version focuses on the core experience: helping a user create a pay cycle plan, track what has been paid, and keep their data backed up.

🏦

Income

Main payday income, extra income, cash income and one-off payments.

📅

Planner

Upcoming bills, payday countdown, checklist and automatic pay cycle reset logic.

💳

Debts

Debt balance tracking, regular payments, extra payments and payoff estimates.

☁️

Cloud backup

Firebase Authentication and Firestore backup for user budget data.

Technology stack

Built with practical mobile development tools

The app is developed with React Native and Expo, tested through Android Studio emulator and Expo development builds, and connected to Firebase for authentication and cloud backup.
