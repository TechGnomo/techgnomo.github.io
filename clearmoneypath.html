Monthly budgets do not work for everyone.

If you get paid weekly or fortnightly, a monthly spreadsheet can feel disconnected from real life. ClearMoneyPath is built around the money that actually lands in your account on payday.

🧾

Bills arrive unevenly

Rent, fuel, loans, insurance, cards and subscriptions rarely line up nicely. ClearMoneyPath helps you prepare before they hit.

💳

Debt needs a plan

See which debt to focus on and track your progress every pay cycle using snowball, avalanche or custom strategies.

🛟

Safe spending matters

The app separates money you can spend from money already needed for bills, debt payments, savings and future expenses.

3
1

Set your payday

Choose whether you are paid weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Add your income amount and the app builds your pay cycle plan.

2

Add bills, debts and savings

Enter what must be paid, what you want to save and which debts you are clearing. ClearMoneyPath converts everything into the current pay cycle.

3

Spend with clarity

Log spending during the cycle and watch your remaining safe-to- spend balance update, so you know where you stand before payday.

Built for real paydays, not perfect spreadsheets.

ClearMoneyPath brings together the core tools people actually need when money is tight, bills are coming and debt needs attention.

💰

Income planning

Track payday income, extra income and one-off payments in one simple cycle view.

📅

Bills tracker

Add due days, categories and paid status. See what is overdue, due today and coming soon.

🎯

Debt focus

Choose snowball, avalanche or custom payoff order and track payments cycle by cycle.

🧠

Safe-to-spend

See what is left after bills, debts, reserves, savings and logged spending.

🏦

Sinking funds

Plan for yearly, quarterly or large future costs by saving a small amount each pay cycle.

☁️

Cloud backup

Login with email or Google and save your budget backup securely to your private account.
