The problem

A bank balance is not a spending plan.

People get paid, cover immediate bills and try to remember everything else coming before the next payday. The account balance looks available even when much of it already has a job.

Traditional monthly budgets can make that harder for weekly and fortnightly income. ClearMoneyPath treats each pay cycle as the planning unit and protects future obligations before calculating what is genuinely free.

The planning system

Every dollar gets a job before the leftover.

Income, bills, debts and savings are organised into one pay-cycle view so the user can make everyday decisions without rebuilding the budget in their head.

  1. 01

    Start at payday

    Choose a weekly, fortnightly or monthly cycle and set the income available for that period.

  2. 02

    Reserve obligations

    Protect upcoming bills, required debt payments and the savings target before treating money as available.

  3. 03

    See the safe number

    Calculate what remains for flexible spending across the days left in the current pay cycle.

  4. 04

    Track the longer path

    Keep debts, progress and future goals visible without losing the immediate payday decision.
